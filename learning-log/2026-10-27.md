## Learning

An attractive individual result can still be a redundant portfolio addition. Current eligibility and differentiated research quality should be tracked separately. Previously inspected test data is historical validation evidence, not a fresh test.

## Open questions

How can I measure incremental portfolio contribution reliably? Which timing and missing-data assumptions materially change results? These require evidence rather than guesses.
