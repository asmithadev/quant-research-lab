# Alpha research learnings

Record general research lessons here without publishing actual alpha expressions, saved identifiers or submission results.

## A simple note template

```markdown
# A specific research question

Date: YYYY-MM-DD

## Question
What am I trying to understand?

## Reasoning
Why might the relationship exist? What could explain it instead?

## Method
Describe a shareable process, not a restricted formula or dataset.

## Learning
What did the exercise actually establish?

## Limitations and next step
What remains uncertain? What useful test comes next?
```

An untested idea belongs under “question” or “reasoning,” not “learning.”
